Galaxy demo

1,000 stars, every planet and moon generated from one seed and the rules in js/rules.js.

Zoom: scroll or pinch. Zooming toward a star or planet locks onto it.
Pan: drag.
Fly: move the mouse near a star, planet or moon: a dotted yellow ring goes round it and stays a moment, so fast moons are easy to catch. Click while it shows to fly there. On touch screens, double-tap.
Keys: H home · G galaxy · S walkers · P stats · T terrain look · B blended borders · +/− zoom

Terrain switches between the new planet-surface look (shaded hills, deeper water with surf, natural biome edges) and the old flat one, to compare.

Rocket takes you to the launch pad at Home's North Pole (or click the pad or any rocket to fly it). Space starts the engine, Q/E throttle, A/D turn, R brings the camera back to your rocket, Tab switches rocket, F follows it. A new rocket is built 10 s after each launch. Stay above Home's hazy glow to escape air drag.

Scale opens sliders for the distance ladder: Home's size, Home to Moon, Home to Sun, and the Sun to its nearest star.

Walkers adds thousands of animated people to the planet you're on, to test speed.

Tap anywhere to close.
