ONE CIVILISATION: every building belongs to the same people and the same world. Older tiers are warm and
hand-built (timber, stone, thatch, clay tile); newer tiers keep a little of that warmth (brick, sandstone-coloured
concrete, bronze-tinted glass) so a city of skyscrapers still sits naturally beside its village outskirts.
PALETTE: warm limestone #D8CBB0, whitewash #ECE6D8, terracotta roof #B5583A, slate roof #4E5660,
thatch #B39A62, timber brown #6B4A32, red brick #9A4E3A, warm concrete #B9B1A2, steel grey #7D8790,
glass blue-grey #6F8A9C with warm reflections #C9B48A.
FACING: each building stands square to the camera, its front wall facing the viewer straight on (not turned at
45 degrees), so the front wall and the roof seen from above are both visible. A roughly square footprint.
DETAIL: windows, doors, chimneys, roof tiles and roof clutter are visible but not fussy; it must still read when
drawn small, about the size of a tree in the game.
AVOID: people, cars, signs with text, fences, gardens, trees or ground around the building, fantasy or
sci-fi shapes, neon.
